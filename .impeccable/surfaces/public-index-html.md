# Link de agendamento (público)

Scope: the public booking page opened from the Instagram bio, plus its return-link variant (`?retorno=<token>`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: car owner on a phone, inside Instagram's in-app browser. Job: pick a service, see a slot that is really free, book it without messaging anyone. Action: the service table and the open slots themselves, in working form, near the first viewport. Proof: real services with time and price (example data until the owner edits them); no testimonials, ratings or client counts exist and none may be invented. Constraints: pt-BR, R$, one business (HC Estética Automotiva, Cidade Tabajara, Olinda-PE), Neon Postgres data layer, WhatsApp-only contact.

Memorable moment: confirming gilds the appointment: the date appears in gold and a polishing shine sweeps across it, and the card says when the car should come back.

Unresolved: real services and prices; photos of finished work; online deposit; real backend.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the shop's own gilded sign on black lacquer: the service table set like a sign board with gold prices, open slots drawn as silver outlines, the chosen slot filled in gold. It refuses the category default of a dark "premium detailing" page built around a glossy stock car photo and a generic calendar widget.

OWN-WORLD: Brand pinned by the owner (PRODUCT.md): the HC logo, black, metallic gold and silver, and a wide heavy oblique sans. Translation of the rolled "Muro de letreiro" world into those materials, named: cobalt plaster wall → warm black lacquer with a soft overhead sheen; lime-white brush lettering → gold-leaf lettering with the logo's chrome horizon (metallic gradient, dark drop shade) on display lines only; chalk → silver hairline outlines; paint → solid gold. Kept from the roll: the sign-board service table with dotted leaders, outline = open / solid = chosen, the brush check per finished step, the one-gesture confirm, the gilded confirmation ritual, guide hairlines flanking labels (the logo's own gold rules). Saira throughout: italic expanded black for display, tracked silver caps for labels, regular for text. Chips and buttons lean like the logo's italics.

STORY: The visitor recognizes the logo they saw on Instagram, reads what is done there and for how much, sees which times are open, picks one, leaves a name, WhatsApp and car, and watches the booking turn gold. They leave knowing the date, the place, and when the car should come back.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone, 390 wide. Top: the round HC emblem (184px), then address (Olinda) and today's hours in silver. Directly below, the service table: each row a sign line with service name in silver italic caps, dotted gold leader, price in gold, duration and cycle in small caps; the whole row tappable. "Próximo livre" sits beside the table heading with a hand-drawn arrow.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3 of 7 (hand-painted car-wash and garage lettering by sign painters), seed key db644ca5, materials translated to the owner's pinned brand (gilded sign on black). Raises kept: outline versus fill selection (sewing pattern), guide rules (teletext), size carries hierarchy (Massin), one-gesture confirm (ebru), brush check per finished step (coffeehouse), return link arrives pre-filled (sleeping city).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
